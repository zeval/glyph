Concept notes

The mark is drawn as a stroke-built, single-source SVG. The bowl reads as a lowercase g at distance, while the hard terminal cuts echo constrained handheld UI chrome.

The cyan path is intentionally heavier than the highlight so 32 px launcher and save-data exports keep a clear silhouette.
