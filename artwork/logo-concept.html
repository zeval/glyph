Shape

The main stroke reads as a lowercase g, but the squared terminals and rail give it a device/UI feel instead of a soft book-brand feel.

Exports

Use the inline SVG as the source for future square app icons, PSP package artwork, and README/social preview assets.

Constraints

The design avoids external fonts and dependencies. It should remain legible when reduced to PSP menu and repository favicon sizes.
